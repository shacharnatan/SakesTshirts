Sales Performance by Cities and Most Popular Colors 📊🌈

What's in This Dashboard? 🔍

This dashboard visualizes your t-shirt sales performance across different cities and highlights the most popular colors among your customers. It includes bar charts distribution by region that has the higgest sales, bar charts of faviorte color by amount of orders. 🗺️📊

Business Value 💡

Understand which cities drive your sales to optimize inventory and marketing efforts. Identify trending colors to prioritize in your designs and stock. For example, if blue t-shirts are a hit in New York, you can target similar markets with tailored campaigns! 🌆👕

Use Case Example 🚀

Use this data to plan regional promotions, adjust stock levels based on color popularity, or launch limited-edition colors in high-performing cities to boost sales. 🎉
