Revenue Segmentation by Gender and Customer Acquisition Sources 💸🎯

What's in This Dashboard? 🔍

This dashboard provides a detailed breakdown of your t-shirt revenue by gender (male, female, other) and customer acquisition sources (e.g., social media, referrals, ads). It features stacked bar charts for us to know who is the bigger spender acording gender, bar charts for acquisition channels to know. 📈💰

Business Value 💡

Identify which gender contributes most to your revenue and which acquisition channels are most effective. This helps you allocate marketing budgets wisely and tailor campaigns to specific demographics. For instance, if females drive more sales via Instagram, you can double down on Instagram ads targeting women! 📱👗

Use Case Example 🚀

Use this data to optimize ad spend, refine your marketing strategy by gender, or partner with influencers on high-performing platforms to attract more customers. 🎉
